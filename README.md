## One Star for Monday

A one-star restaurant review of Monday, written as if Monday were a restaurant: the narrator comes in for breakfast, waits forty minutes, and gets served a cold alarm, dead coffee, and a 9 a.m. meeting instead of food. Never again. One star.

The entry is a single line of 24 words, short enough to retype or screenshot into a group chat without trimming. `main.py` writes that exact line, with no trailing newline, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
